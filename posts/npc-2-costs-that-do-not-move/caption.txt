A 6% sales drop and a 5% wage rise. Watch what happens to one store.

Example numbers, our own illustration: one store, one week, $25,000 in sales. Sales fall 6% and wages rise 5%. Prime cost moves from 61.2% to 64.9%. After rent and the store's share of the debt payment, the store goes from $1,200 a week to minus $90.

Now multiply by every store in your group. Rent and debt don't move when sales do; only the costs tied to sales do.

Every number here is an example, not a reported figure.
