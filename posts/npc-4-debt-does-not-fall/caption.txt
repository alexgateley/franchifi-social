Debt payments don't fall when sales fall.

NPC International, once the largest Pizza Hut franchisee in the US, changed hands three times and by 2020 carried about $900 million of debt, according to reports at the time. In early 2020 it missed an interest payment, its credit rating was cut, and it borrowed $35 million in emergency financing.

For a franchise owner the lesson is the shape of the costs: debt payments and rent stay the same when sales drop. Only the costs tied to sales move.

Reported figures: Restaurant Dive, CFO.com, UPI and CNBC (2020); ownership history from Pizza Marketplace and a 2012 SEC filing.
