Prime cost in 30 seconds.

Example numbers for one store, one week:
Sales: $25,000
Cost of goods (food, packaging, drinks): $7,500
Total labor (wages, overtime, payroll taxes, benefits): $7,800
$7,500 + $7,800 = $15,300. Divided by $25,000 = 61.2%.

Many operators aim for roughly 55 to 65 percent, depending on the format. If you own more than one store, do it weekly, store by store.

Sources for the definition and range: Restaurant365, "How to Calculate Prime Cost in a Restaurant", and Mirus, "How to Calculate Restaurant Prime Cost".
