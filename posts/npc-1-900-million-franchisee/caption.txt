The largest Pizza Hut franchisee in the US went bankrupt in 2020.

More than 1,200 Pizza Huts, nearly 400 Wendy's and about $900 million of debt. Its Chapter 11 filing named three pressures: no sales growth, rising costs, and that debt.

For a franchise owner, all three meet in one number: prime cost, food plus labor as a percent of sales. When sales fall, labor, rent and the debt payment don't fall with them, so prime cost climbs and a store can start losing money before anyone looks.

Reported figures: CNBC, Restaurant Dive, CFO.com and KSHB (July 2020). None of them reported NPC's prime cost.
